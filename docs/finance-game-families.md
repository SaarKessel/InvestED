# Shared finance game families

Unify the workflow and components, not distinct professional rules.

- Investment desk: portfolio management, investment advisory and trading exercises can share price inspection, orders, risk and client communication. Preserve authority/suitability differences rather than equating roles.
- Research and valuation: equity analysis and fund research can share evidence, calculations and committee decisions. Keep issuer statements separate from fund holdings and fees.
- Accounting and reporting: accounting, financial control and reporting can share statement review and board explanation.
- Operations desk: securities back-office and mutual-fund back-office share an exception queue, cash/position reconciliation, duplicate controls, source evidence and closing review. Instrument-specific valuation and settlement states remain visible.
- Risk and compliance: these can share review queues but must keep distinct risk, legal and policy bases. Do not imply a game is a professional compliance check.

## First merged operations exercise

One invented operations queue containing securities and mutual-fund records. The player chooses Match, Hold or Escalate from a documented fictional control card. Compare cash, units and identifiers; pending fund NAV blocks matching; duplicate identifiers go to review. No invented market-wide settlement calendar or NAV cutoff. The control card is a rule for this case, not a statement of current law or industry practice.

Use the existing read-only source desk for price inspection, clearly separated from case ledger values. For new runs, finish only when each exception is classified correctly, supported by the matching fictional evidence packet and explicitly resolved. Preserve original discrepancies for review. Closed legacy classification-only runs remain readable and are labeled as such. Store only device-local progress; no actual settlement, transfer, cash instruction or professional verification.

## Shared committee cases

Investment, research and reporting each use the same one-case-at-a-time committee module with four role-specific cases and numerical evidence. Review and defer are distinct; approval is correct only for a narrow claim supported by the provided facts. No committee choice changes the main fictional ledger or creates a real-world recommendation. Device-local conflicts require explicit loading of the newer saved snapshot before a later choice can be saved.

New operations runs add a combined position/cash break requiring one packet that supports both corrections. Exercises are versioned locally: older six-record runs finish under their original controls; restart begins the seven-record version. Archive counts reflect the exercise actually completed.
