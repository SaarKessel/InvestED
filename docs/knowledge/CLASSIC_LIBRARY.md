# Economics models and book ideas in InvestED

## Scope

The existing concept registry now includes 13 economics-model notes and 9 book-idea syntheses in Hebrew and English. Each note includes attribution, the idea, why it matters, a learning exercise, limitations and public source links. Existing concept graph links connect them to finance topics. The same stored explanations support named questions, retrieval fallback, related concepts and term explanations.

This is an educational content expansion. It does not train or replace the language model, retrieve full books, implement new financial calculations, or guarantee an investment outcome. No new network service, account, paid source or API key is involved.

The notes are written from scratch in InvestED's voice. They do not reproduce passages, stories, chapter structures or close paraphrases. The book notes expressly cover selected public ideas rather than claim to be complete summaries. Practice prompts and critiques are InvestED commentary, not quotations or personal advice.

## Models

- Markowitz: portfolio selection and correlation.
- Sharpe: CAPM, distinct from the existing Sharpe-ratio entry.
- Kahneman and Tversky: prospect theory. Tversky is credited as a contributor, not a prize recipient.
- Thaler: behavioural economics, mental accounting and choice design. Sunstein is credited for Nudge, not called a laureate.
- Black, Scholes and Merton: option pricing. Black is credited as a contributor, not a prize recipient.
- Fama: efficient markets, with assumptions and caveats.
- Shiller: excessive price movements and exuberance, not crash timing.
- Tobin: portfolio choice and links between finance and real spending.
- Modigliani and Miller: capital structure under explicit idealised assumptions.
- Modigliani and Brumberg: life-cycle saving.
- Spence: signaling.
- Akerlof: adverse selection and the market for lemons.
- Stiglitz: screening.

## Books

The Intelligent Investor; Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits; One Up on Wall Street; The Psychology of Money; A Random Walk Down Wall Street; Security Analysis; The Lean Startup; Zero to One; Good to Great.

Hebrew titles are learning labels and search aliases, not claims about an authorised translation or edition. This library contains no current prices, trading signals, country-specific legal instructions or return forecasts.
